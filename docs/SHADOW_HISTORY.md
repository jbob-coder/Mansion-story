## Witnesses and disputed memory
The Unbound Charter is an objective legal act; claims that beastfolk caused the Scar are **false accusations**, maintained in prejudiced factions as `rumor`. The Night Archivist is real, but its ability to manipulate individuals is limited and the scope of its influence is unproven. Later chroniclers are not omniscient.

## Source and historical limit
The named episodes above are **authored seed outcomes**, not evidence of an operating civilization model. The initial year 30,000 snapshot is paused: the ledger ends before the next outcome is decided.
