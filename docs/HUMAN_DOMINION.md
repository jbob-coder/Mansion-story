# The Human Dominion — Canon of the High Banners

**World:** Eirenfall (`WORLD-001`), current authored era Year 30,000. **Authorial decision:** humans are the most dominant sapient species in the **known inhabited world**. The creator has **not** dictated future victory, permanent moral privilege or species extinction.

## What dominance means
- **Population:** among ten surveyed/estimated settlement service areas, 64,520 of 80,850 sapient residents (79.8%) are humans (`SPC-005`). These are **authored estimates**, not a simulated census and not a true globe-wide headcount.
- **Infrastructure:** human-founded crown cities, granaries, bridges, farms and road systems are extensive.
- **Sovereignty:** Vael Taryn (`POL-001`) and Bellward March (`POL-002`) impose taxes and secure routes with comparatively large human-centered institutions.
- **Military:** large supply chains, disciplined garrisons and defended roads create territorial influence. They do not grant humans automatic victory in every duel or encounter.
- **Trade and administration:** humans operate the most pervasive recorded charter, literate courts, granaries and merchant institutions; mixed-species trade remains indispensable.
- **Historical reach:** this is a result of documented migrations, farming, treaties, opportunism, alliances and conquest pressure, not supernatural racial destiny.

## The ascent is historical, not predestined
- Year 17,120 (`EVT-070`): mobile proto-human communities share tool traditions and knowledge.
- Year 21,500 (`EVT-071`): movement through the valleys links families, resources and safe paths.
- Year 27,400 (`EVT-072`): cultivated land, animal care, dry stores and river transport expand.
- Year 28,700 (`EVT-073`): grain compacts fund roads, bridges and market protections.
- Year 28,900 (`EVT-074`): the earliest comparative records show human leading infrastructure in documented heartlands.
- Year 29,430 (`EVT-050`): Crownspire City receives a durable civic charter.
- Year 29,520 (`EVT-006`): Vael Taryn's crown unifies further political institutions.
- Year 29,950 (`EVT-067`) and 29,999 (`EVT-069`): survey offices revise demographic estimates.

This chronology does **not** establish that humanity was always the majority or created first. The earliest sapient species chronology remains open; do not rewrite it without evidence.

## Humans are not a uniform faction
They may be farmers, scribes, knights, exiles, fugitives, mercenaries, kings, exploiters, opponents of captivity or ordinary neighbors. `SPC-005` has **Bond** as a descriptive species-level reference affinity. Each individual human's First Thread remains exactly the existing **one** domain assigned to that person (Stone, Sight, Hearth, Verdance, etc.). Species has no power to overwrite an individual's ability.

Human states differ: Crownspire's aristocratic customs are not all citizens' beliefs. Human-led unjust laws can be resisted by human and nonhuman residents. The Gloam Chain commits deliberate abuse; the Unbound Watch resists it, and both include humans.

## Other peoples do not lose agency
- **Hornborn `SPC-003`:** Stonehorn Camp (`SET-002`) is a sapient frontier community with land claims.
- **Deepkin `SPC-006`:** the Brackenhold Enclave (`SET-005`) and Free Assembly (`POL-004`) maintain a self-governing territory and craft economy.
- **Reedfolk `SPC-007`:** the Reedfen Stiltward (`SET-006`) participates in the River League (`POL-003`); waterways and navigation create bargaining power.
- Species can live in mixed settlements and belong to multiple traditions.

No society's political strength determines moral worth. Individuals can cooperate or conflict across species. Some regions have a nonhuman local majority even though humans dominate the surveyed **aggregate**.

## Known geopolitical hierarchy
`POL-001` Vael Taryn is largest among the currently recorded states. `POL-002` Bellward March is its vassal. `POL-003` Reedfen River League and `POL-004` Brackenhold Free Assembly possess different authority structures. No territorial polygons or globe-wide empire inventory are known, and all three must be handled separately from species identity.

**Important limit:** dominance is canon for the documented human-known sphere, while undiscovered continents and deeper ages remain unanswered. The creator's chosen premise remains true without inventing a statistically impossible universal count.
