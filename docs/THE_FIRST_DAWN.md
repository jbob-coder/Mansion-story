# The First Dawn — Genesis Canon

**Provenance:** authored foundational canon; the deity's perspective below is a literary framing, not testimony from an in-game character.

## Before counting
There was the Unmeasured: no separation, time, light, or witness. The creator did not place a king, choose a hero, or write an ending. The first act was to grant reality the capacity to change without seeking permission.

At `EVT-000`, year **0**, an interval became distinct from another interval. Time began. A world-space (`WORLD-001`) took form around the Axis of Stillness, and the surface, deep, sky and Veil became distinguishable.

At `EVT-001`, year **600**, heat and pressure shaped the first enduring stone. The creator did not design every mountain; instead, matter could fracture, accumulate and erode.

At `EVT-002`, year **1,800**, water found inclines. Seas and rivers formed from flow and terrain rather than a decree prescribing every course. Charted rivers in later ages are outcomes, not predestined objects.

At `EVT-003`, year **6,200**, the first self-sustaining life spread across sheltered rock and tide pools. Every new being carried one **First Thread** — a single core capacity that could differentiate through environment, inheritance, practice and transformation. Inert matter, places and crafted objects also had one latent affinity domain; latency was not consciousness.

At `EVT-004`, year **16,000**, some creatures became able to imagine an alternative to what stood before them. Awareness made cooperation, refusal, deception, care and invention possible. No god-command chose whom they would love, where they would settle, or whom they would fight.

Later populations altered the landscape: fires cleared woodland, trails became roads, tracks became borders, and old promises became laws. These changes were made by agents inside the world. A creator can describe the founding era in canon, but a future live simulation must **record actual agent actions** rather than replay a predetermined saga.

## Five creator constraints
1. **Consistency:** yesterday constrains today. Magic has costs and a recorded mechanism.
2. **Choice:** sapient agents have competing motives and incomplete knowledge; they are not assigned a story destiny.
3. **Consequence:** extraction, construction, wars, illness, restoration, and migration leave material traces.
4. **Continuity:** a name, lineage, ruin, road or historical event has an ID and audit trail.
5. **Discovery:** unseen land, unidentified realms and uncertain histories remain unknown until established by evidence.

## Myth versus ontology
Priests may describe the First Dawn differently. Their accounts belong to religious or cultural belief records, not necessarily objective cosmology. The above is the out-of-world design baseline. A living character can be wrong about it.
