# The Shadows of Eirenfall — Evil, Choice, and Consequence

**Status:** out-of-world authored canon; the following history is not a simulated event trace.

## Creation did not command morality
Eirenfall's creator gave each being a First Thread and permitted a future that was not prewritten. Cooperation, courage, mercy, self-interest, cruelty, deceit, betrayal, and domination became possible once sentient life could choose between alternatives. The creator **allows evil to exist**; the creator does not mark entire species, bloodlines, cultures or ability domains as evil.

Three separate things are often called "darkness":
1. **Moral evil:** a sapient actor knowingly commits severe avoidable harm or coerces another being, especially for gain, status or pleasure.
2. **Ecological danger:** hunting, infection, storm, hunger, animal aggression, predation. Harmful outcomes do not automatically imply moral wrongdoing.
3. **Supernatural malignancy:** a real hostile agency or scar left in the Veil. It has mechanisms, limits, and a history; it is not the source of every wrong act.

Someone can be a villain in one person's account and a protector in another's. Objective actions, informed consent, harms and lost opportunities are recorded separately from an observer's judgment. Motive explains a choice without magically excusing it.

## The First Betrayal — year 18,420
At the Old Cairn, Esh Vel (`NPC-007`), a gifted Binder of the Bond Thread, practiced coordinated work before discovering a way to **fasten pain to promises**. At `EVT-020`, Vel forced a captive's compliance without the captive's consent. The victim's identity is currently **unknown**. Their voice was suppressed; histories written by Vel's successors falsely described the act as salvation.

This was neither the first injury nor proof that prior centuries were innocent. It is the **earliest documented deliberate Binding abuse** in the current archive.

The rite damaged the Veil. In year 18,424 (`EVT-043`), a self-aware predatory memory-being, **the Night Archivist** (`ENT-001`), emerged from accumulated stolen recollections. It chooses to lure mortals toward isolation and betrayal, but it cannot make choices for them. It inhabits the Veil, not a false point on a surface map. Its **one core ability is Echo** (`ABI-007`).

## The four social engines of cruelty
- **Need:** scarcity encourages raiding, scapegoating and dangerous bargains, while creating opportunities for mutual aid.
- **Power:** authority can protect lives or concentrate violence; lawfulness and morality are not synonyms.
- **Fear:** an unknown beast or a different-looking person can become a convenient symbol for disaster.
- **Profit:** forced labor, contraband magical bindings and illegal capture create incentives to hide victims and corrupt institutions.

None of these functions as a fixed "evil-meter" determining a character's future actions.

## Living agents of darkness, year 30,000
- **The Gloam Chain (`ORG-002`)** traffics in coercive magical oaths, uses concealed pens, and profits from controlling others. Its members have varying motives; the institution commits real harm.
- **Varek Sorn (`NPC-008`)**, a senior organizer, deliberately uses `SUB-017` Chain Oath despite knowing its consequences. An unrepentant starting-state antagonist, but still not immune to capture, regret or death.
- **Yorren Pike (`NPC-012`)** uses tracking skills to help illegal capture; economic need explains his complicity but does not erase accountability.
- **The Night Archivist (`ENT-001`)** hunts memories and cultivates distrust. Its agency makes it more than a natural disaster.
- **The Crown's Hide Levy (`LAW-002`)** is lawful yet drives suffering, poaching and discriminatory enforcement. A kingdom need not be a wholly evil faction to operate an unjust law.

## Resistance and imperfect goodness
The Unbound Watch (`ORG-003`) rescues captives and records evidence, but its members debate restitution versus retribution. The Ashenwood Hunting Circle (`ORG-004`) alternately protects ecological balance and helps enforce harmful levy requirements. The same person may rescue a child and harm a rival. Honest history preserves both facts.

**No guaranteed redemption:** villainous acts have durable consequences; repentance requires action and cannot undo deaths by author decree. **No guaranteed damnation:** future choice remains open unless an actor's capacities are demonstrably removed.

## Nonnegotiable world rules
- No First Thread or species is born with a moral alignment.
- Coercive magic cannot erase agency for free; `SUB-017` causes pain, exhaustion and a breakable tether, **not total control of decisions**.
- `ENT-001` is an actual intelligent hostile Veil inhabitant; other supposed evil deities remain religious claims until independently established.
- A wolf eating prey is not murder. A person deliberately abusing a captive is accountable.
- Institutions and laws can be structurally harmful even if individual participants mean well.
- All developments after the paused Year 30,000 snapshot must be actual simulation events or explicitly new authored decisions, never passed off as already happened.
