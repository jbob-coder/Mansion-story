# The First Thread — Universal Core Ability System

## Invariant: one origin, many branches
Every registered world entity, including stone, tree, river site, artifact, institution and person, owns **exactly one core ability domain** (`core_ability_id`). The domain is not always actively supernatural. For a rock, a stone domain can express stability and fractures; it does not grant thoughts or freedom of movement. For a guild, its domain describes organizational aptitude, not a separate living soul.

Domains are **shared types**, but each entity has its own identity, experience, restrictions and unlock state. Multiple individuals can have `ABI-001` without having the same personal capability. An entity cannot replace its one core domain by gaining an unrelated second core; any transformation requires an explicitly authorized, history-recorded metamorphosis which still leaves exactly one active domain.

## Initial ability domains
| ID | Domain | Example unlock paths |
|---|---|---|
| ABI-001 | Hearth | heat tending -> ember shaping -> wildfire restraint |
| ABI-002 | Stone | material reading -> masonry bond -> fault sense |
| ABI-003 | Current | water sense -> channel shaping -> current riding |
| ABI-004 | Verdance | seed tending -> root weaving -> regenerative bloom |
| ABI-005 | Sight | trail reading -> distant focus -> pattern inference |
| ABI-006 | Bond | shared labor -> oath memory -> coordinated resolve |
| ABI-007 | Echo | resonance listening -> memory trace -> warded signal |
| ABI-008 | Wind | air reading -> sail guiding -> storm listening |

Each `SUB` ID names an **unlockable sub-ability under exactly one ABI**; specific prerequisites and costs are registered in the data. Unlocks may be latent or discovered; they are not granted merely by being important to the story.

## Unlock mechanics (design contract)
A proposal to unlock needs: (1) parent core domain match, (2) prerequisite sub-abilities where any, (3) evidence of the required skill, experience or environmental circumstance, (4) viable energy/material/health cost, and (5) a recorded event. No fixed class railroad or story chapter is required. Learning can fail or regress. Non-sapient entities can manifest a passive ability due to environmental conditions without intentional study.

## Power limitations
- Power does **not** violate material permanence for free; every effect requires a cost, transference or transformation.
- An ability does not itself create autonomy. A sword's Echo resonance is not a thinking sword unless distinct sentience emerges and is recorded.
- Ability ranks are descriptive proficiency, not hereditary nobility or political authority.
- Destruction and crafting must register altered materials, ownership and world state.
- Players and NPCs follow the same physics and ability rules; privilege is not hidden in plot armor.

## Example
`NPC-005` (Tessa Flint) holds **Hearth** as the single core domain and may unlock `SUB-001` *Ember Tending* and then `SUB-002` *Forge Heat Shaping*. She cannot casually learn Stone as a second core merely because she works with metal; she must solve stone problems using tools, collaboration and techniques accessible through her existing domain.

## Forbidden or dangerous branches, not evil core domains
- `SUB-017` **Chain Oath** (Bond `ABI-006`; requires `SUB-011`): a painful, resistible, forbidden coercive tether. Choosing to use it on unwilling sapient captives is culpable harm; it grants **neither another core** nor total control of the victim's mind.
- `SUB-018` **Witness Scar** (Echo `ABI-007`; requires `SUB-013`): extracts fragmented traumatic traces at real personal cost, with uncertainty and possible misinterpretation.
- `SUB-019` **Thorn Shelter** (Verdance `ABI-004`; requires `SUB-007`): protective growth responsive to available seeds and nutrients; conservation is not free matter creation.

The Night Archivist also follows the one-core rule: `ABI-007` Echo. A being or artifact that produces terrifying effects does not receive a second hidden domain. No branch has a built-in good/evil flag: **what a person does with an ability, to whom, and with whose consent** matters.
